# Power-saving staff review and fulfillment batch

Canonical scope: `03-core-business.md#T-03.09.04.01`–`.02`, staff review and rejection from S-03.09.04, and `03-core-business.md#T-03.10.01.01`–`.05`. This batch follows the order-intake batch and completes the review-to-fulfillment journey. Documents/comments (`E-03.10.02`) and inventory (`E-03.10.03`) remain separate batches.

Staff can review the saving-order queue and a single order's submitted plan, price, address, bill identifier, invoice, contract, five stages and immutable stage history. Approving publishes the exact contract version, confirms the order, completes request confirmation and starts product delivery. Rejecting requires a reason, cancels an unpaid invoice and releases its gift-code slot, or creates a durable wallet refund obligation for a paid invoice. Both decisions use staff permission, step-up verification, idempotency, current-version checks, audit and customer notification.

The five canonical stages are request confirmation, product delivery, installation and document upload, optional equipment handover, and process completion. Staff record an explanation when completing a stage. Equipment handover can instead be skipped, or completed with a required item description. Advancement is ordered and recorded with previous/new state, actor and time. Delivery requires a paid invoice; process completion requires a paid invoice and active contract. Completed and rejected orders reject further advancement. Customers see the current stage, completed/skipped stages, dates, and handover details on the order detail page.

Migration 0162 maps the earlier placeholder stage names, adds stage audit fields and an append-only event table, extends unpublished contract rejection to saving orders, and makes the existing refund obligation reference the shared order table. The refund worker verifies saving-order rejection evidence before crediting the wallet and links its completion notice to the saving order.

Validation: migrated HTTP flow covering submission, staff queue, approval/retry/conflict, payment and contract acceptance, ordered advancement, optional skip, terminal guard, customer progress, unpaid rejection, paid rejection, wallet credit, and refund notice. API and web builds/typechecks, database snapshot, OpenAPI contract, formatting, lint, i18n and web tests were run for this batch. The pre-existing bundle budget failure on `main` remains outside this batch.
