## Delivered

Wallet history supports literal search across transaction IDs, public references/descriptions and original bank receipt bank names/transfer references. Private decision metadata and copied credit receipt fields are excluded from search. Search, type/state, order, account-zone dates and exact inclusive IRR magnitude bounds combine in one query. Magnitude includes both credits and debits, preserves amounts above JavaScript's safe integer and avoids overflow at the minimum stored bigint debit. The existing inclusive API `to` bound remains supported; new UI ranges use the exclusive `until` bound. Calendar/offset validation and full fractional instant comparisons reject malformed or reversed dates. Live profile permissions, final session locks and curated response fields remain intact.

The shared drawer applies search/order/date/amount/type/state edits atomically, retains invalid amount drafts and discards Cancelled edits. Chips remove individual criteria; Clear retains order. Namespaced `history_` URLs preserve exact amount strings, opaque cursors, reload and Back independently of gateway/invoice return parameters. Pages are bounded to 25 transactions, tied PostgreSQL microseconds remain exact and legacy cursor hashes are retained without new criteria. Search/profile changes reject obsolete reads and clear profile-bound continuation. Failed-page Retry keeps its exact cursor and accepted rows; lost authorization clears private rows. Repeated continuation cannot advance. Filtering preserves unsubmitted top-up fields and existing financial reviews.

The date picker loads when the drawer opens. Its new 60 KB interaction budget measures 52.09 KB. All previous limits remain unchanged; wallet is 266.02 KB / 300 KB and electricity ordering is 247.56 KB / 255 KB.
