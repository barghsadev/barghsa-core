# Contract initial-invoice next action

Canonical scope: customer payment guidance within `04-invoices-wallet-contracts.md#T-04.5.03.03`.

An accepted or signed customer contract with an unpaid, partially funded, or overdue initial invoice now points its next-action banner to that invoice. When a submitted payment is under review, the banner explains the staff wait instead. Acceptance and signature actions keep priority, and completed contracts have no next action. The labels work in English and Persian.
