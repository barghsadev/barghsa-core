# Shared dependent address selection, October 2, 2026

## Scope and result

This related profile-form batch implements `07-ui-ux-design.md#T-07.10.02.04`. The shared UI `DependentSelect` filters choices by the controlling field and supports asynchronous readiness/loading supplied by the existing geography hook. Personal onboarding, company official/representative addresses, CRM customer addresses and CRM company official/representative addresses adopt it together: six live city fields across four forms.

Changing province clears the form's city selection. City results are validated and bound to their requested province; obsolete reads are cancelled and cannot restore another province's choices. Missing parents, loading and failed reads disable selection. Existing retry controls preserve unrelated form fields and restored draft city IDs. Saved inactive CRM cities remain visible under their original province, without becoming selectable for a new address. Server validation, permission checks, confirmation, version checks and step-up authentication remain in place.

The shared component supports synchronous option groups without fetching. Geography fetching, cancellation and recovery reuse `useGeographyOptions`; no second request mechanism, dependency, endpoint or migration is introduced. Other address forms in electricity, saving, profile settings and address settings still use their existing selectors and are candidates for the next adoption batch. Separate FormStep, dynamic-field-array and other form-system criteria remain open.
