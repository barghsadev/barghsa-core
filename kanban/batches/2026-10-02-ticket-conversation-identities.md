## Delivered behavior

Customer and staff account menus now open a shared Persian/English conversation identity editor. People may choose a name of up to 80 characters and one PNG, JPG or WebP image up to 2 MB, or remove either. The editor explains that these details are shared with other participants in support conversations. Existing accounts keep generic role labels and initials; login email, phone and profile information are never inferred as public identity.

The self-owned GET/PUT endpoint works in both operating contexts without broadening other customer routes. It validates names, rejects control and directional override characters while accepting Persian join controls, and checks live account/session authority under locks. Revision checks prevent conflicting writes; exact lost-acknowledgement retries reuse the saved immutable photo and audit entry. Name-only edits preserve the photo. Explicit removal clears even an unavailable storage reference. Identity changes and audit entries commit atomically; failed image copies retain the existing independent cleanup intent.

Photo presigns are account-scoped and reject profile/ticket associations, oversized declarations and non-image content. Recording and sealing recheck actual bytes, size, uploader, purpose and verification. The existing immutable attachment service copies bounded, sniffed image bytes and signs downloads for 300 seconds. Cross-user, wrong-purpose, unverified and profile-scoped sources are rejected. Images use the existing storage configuration and policy; unavailable storage does not invent a photo.

Ticket reads project only chosen names and verified photo URLs, in one query for the already authorized, visibility-filtered comment set. Customers receive no internal author identity, message or photo. Disabled/inactive authors fall back to the existing role display. Public aliases take precedence over the staff-only directory; role and internal-message badges remain visible. Rendering escapes names and rejects unsafe image URL protocols.

The editor preserves draft names/files and verified uploads across save failures, refreshes revisions after a conflict without replacing the draft, and clears personal drafts on account denial during either saving or photo verification. Unmounted upload operations cannot submit an obsolete edit. The localized photo button supports keyboard file selection and avoids English native picker text in Persian UI.
