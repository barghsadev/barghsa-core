## Review and corrections

Review found that comparing activity with alert time is unsafe: PostgreSQL `NOW()` records transaction start, so a staff transaction can start before the scanner, wait on its source lock, and commit its response after the alert with an earlier timestamp. Consultation episodes now snapshot the source activity at full PostgreSQL precision. Changed snapshots reset the episode; escalation requires exact equality. Source SHARE locks remain held through the ledger/outbox transaction. A real PostgreSQL barrier regression reproduces the reversed timestamp order and verifies both refusal to escalate the old episode and a fresh later breach.
